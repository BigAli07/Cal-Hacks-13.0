# LastPing video script (~90 seconds)

**0–10 s — Problem**

“Weak intermittent satellite signals can disappear into noise and
interference. LastPing tests whether known orbital motion adds useful evidence
without pretending a Doppler match proves identity.”

**10–24 s — Real provenance**

Show real replay mode and click **Analyze recording**.

“This is archived SatNOGS observation 6659851—not a simulation and not live.
It is a hash-verified 2022 FUNCUBE-1 reception from PE0SAT-32 at 145.935 MHz.
The app also validates the original metadata and historical TLE.”

**24–48 s — Visible evidence**

Show the waterfall and legend.

“The waterfall is computed from 16.9 million decoded audio samples. Dashed
lines are orbital predictions, red is the searched best-fit template, and cyan
is an independently extracted measured ridge. Those are deliberately kept
separate.”

**48–66 s — Candidate comparison**

Show candidate table.

“FUNCUBE-1 ranks first with physics score 6.825, a minus-500-hertz residual
offset, and 99.3 percent horizon support. Three historical incorrect satellites
are compared; all are below this receiver’s horizon. SatNOGS independently
published 53 decoded frames for the pass.”

**66–80 s — ML contribution**

Show method cards.

“The logistic model was genuinely trained on 500 synthetic recordings. Its
real score is shown as experimental and uncalibrated; it does not make the real
decision. Processing takes about four seconds, separate from recording time.”

**80–90 s — Honest boundary**

“This demonstrates a traceable real replay and candidate comparison, not live
reception, orbit recovery, or calibrated RF sensitivity. With one real positive
and no verified negative corpus, real false-alarm rate is not measured.”

## Capture instructions

1. Use a 1280×720 or larger browser viewport with the sidebar expanded.
2. Begin on the unprocessed real replay screen.
3. Record the click, spinner, result banner, waterfall legend, candidate table,
   method cards, provenance expander, JSON download button and Reset.
4. Keep the cursor clear of the waterfall labels while narrating.
5. Export at 1080p/H.264, play the entire file once, and verify audio, text
   readability and that no loading spinner remains in the final result shot.

No video file was recorded by Codex; only the rendered application was visually
inspected in-browser.
